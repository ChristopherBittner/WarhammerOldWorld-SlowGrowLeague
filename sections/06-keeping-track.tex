\section{Keeping Track}

The Overseer will be updating a spreadsheet with standings, pairings, and the phases' mission.
Player will need to submit their results to the Overseer.
A result consist of: 
\begin{itemize}
  \item game date, 
  \item players names, 
  \item Victory Points, 
  \item Points for killed units and characters,
  \item Who controlls the treasure (if map had any) 
  \item How much treasure was destroyed (if any)
  \item Was army painted to a battle ready standard (at least 3 colours)
\end{itemize}
Everything else is calculated by the Overseer (CP and EXP).

\subsection{Scoring}

Overseer will compare yous and your opponent difference of victory
points you have earned during the match. Your score is based on this differential, see
table below.

\subsubsection{Victory Points}

We will be using the rules for Victory Points from page~19 of the Match Play Guide.

\subsubsection{Dead or Fled}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Enemy unit destroyed or fled of the battlefield is worth VP equal to 100\% of the cost.
  \item Enemy units fleeing at the end of the battle are worth 50\% of the cost (round up).
  \item An enemy unit that has been reduced to 25\% of its starting Unit Strength or less, or if its Unit Strength is equal to its starting Wounds, to 25\% of its starting Wounds or less, is worth VP equal to 50\% of the cost.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{The King is Dead}

If the general is slain or has fled the battlefield, you earn 100~VP.

\subsubsection{Trophies of War}

You earn 50~VP for every enemy standard claimed as a trophy, page~200 in the rulebook. If the BSB is dead or is fleeing, you earn 50~VP.

\subsection{Points for killed units and characters \& Experience Points}

Experience is tracked as a shared \XPshort{} pool for your army (similar to the Treasure pool).

\begin{itemize}
  \item Gain 1~\XPshort{} for every 100~points of enemy units or characters destroyed
    (e.g.\ destroying 400~points of units grants 4~\XPshort).
  \item Gain 1~\XPshort{} for every 200~points of enemy unit that has been reduced to 25\% of its starting Unit Strength or less.
  \item Gain (or lose) \XPshort{} equal to your Victory Points difference divided by~100.
    On a loss this value is negative.
\end{itemize}

All \XPshort{} earned over the campaign accumulates in this pool. It may only be spent on
options such as Veteran, Poisoned attacks upgrades, the next level of wizard, and similar advancement choices - but not on mounts, armour, or weapon upgrades. Experience points cannot be used together with normal pool, ie. options have to paid fully from one or the other pool.
Every XP point corresponds to 1 point used in muster list.

This system is intended to support progression without forcing identical unit composition
from phase to phase.

\subsection{Treasures}
Treasue is gained by controlling objective on the map at the end of the game.
Treasures creates a separate pool of points you can spend on items. 
You must fully pay for these items from that pool; you cannot combine Treasure points with your core army points.

For example, if you have earned 50 Treasure points, you may buy a single item costing at most~50, or several cheaper items totalling 50~or less. 
You may not buy an item costing~65 by covering the remaining~15 from your core points.

If the character or unit holding a treasure items is destroyed, those items are destroyed and the points are removed from the Treasure pool. 
If the unit survived till end of the game (or flee), the points spent on those items return to the pool for future battles.
