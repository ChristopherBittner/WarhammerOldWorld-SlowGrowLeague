\section{Building an Army}

Below 1000 points:
Prior to the start of the campaign, every participant must each write a 500~points muster list, 
as described in the Battle March rules (see below) and submit this to the Overseer.
Players should exchange muster lists with each other not later than 2 days before the agreed time of the battle. That should happen simultaneously so that players do not influence own list based on the opponent's one.
Above or equal to 1000 points:
Use Grand Melee and Combined Arms rules from page 13 of the Match Play Guide.

Additonal rules:
- WYSIWYG (loadout): If you units are not 100% clear by looking at the models, you are obligated to explain it to your opponent.
- Proxy (models): Your opponent should easily be able to see what the unit is supposed to be, if not ask the Overseer before the game.
- Legacy armies are allowed but must follow Renegade rules to make themselves more on par with current armies.
Link to Renegade Legacy Pack: https://www.squarebased.com/
