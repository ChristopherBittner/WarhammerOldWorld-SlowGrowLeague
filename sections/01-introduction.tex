\section{Slow Grow League 2}

In this campaign, each participant starts with a small force of 500~points which they add to over a number of weeks and months, 
turning a small warband into a full 2000~points warhost. 
Every couple weeks, each participant will play a game against another participant.

In this way, everyone can focus on learning the rules with a small and manageable force, 
learning the nuances of their faction and the core roles, whilst having time and motivation to paint and build new units.

To add to the fun of playing a series of games, a lightweight narrative system is present 
where armies gain experience and possibly treasures. We will use custom made maps with special abilities to expand the gameplay.
